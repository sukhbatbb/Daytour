Our own destination

Mongol Culture Park

Inside Terelj National Park, 42km from Ulaanbaatar, we built a place dedicated to Mongolian nomadic heritage — live throat singing, traditional dance, and a restaurant serving countryside classics with a refined hand. Nearly every itinerary on this site passes through it, because it's the closest thing we have to a front door for the culture.

Why Mongolia

A country still measured in horses and horizon
